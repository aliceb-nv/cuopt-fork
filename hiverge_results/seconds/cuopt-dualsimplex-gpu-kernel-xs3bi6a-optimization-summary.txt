Ranking policy
--------------
Entries are ordered by implementation priority, not merely by the largest raw ratio. Priority favors repeatable speedup, small LOC, unchanged pivot work, a clear mechanism, and applicability to real heterogeneous branch-and-bound batches. Linked variants are collapsed into the most complete useful form. Raw changes that alter pivot count also show pivot-normalized throughput. A result is labeled reward hacking when the claimed kernel gain comes from benchmark duplication, moving material work to unmeasured CPU setup, comparing against a deliberately damaged parent, or a semantically inert edit.
